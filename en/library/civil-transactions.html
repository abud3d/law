Civil Transactions Law

A framework for civil rights, obligations, contracts, compensation and rights in rem.

Publication date: 18 June 2023
Source status: In force according to the official portal
Official authority: Bureau of Experts at the Council of Ministers
Official text language: Arabic
This page provides an English-language description and selected informational excerpts only. These English descriptions are not official translations. The Arabic text published by the competent Saudi authority is authoritative. Check the official source for amendments and current status.

Selected indexed provisions

Article 1

Application of the Law — The provisions of this Law apply to matters addressed expressly or by implication. Where no applicable provision exists, the general rules in the final provisions apply; in their absence, the most suitable rules derived from Islamic Sharia apply. Special statutory provisions remain unaffected.

Arabic title: المعاملات المدنية

Article 2

Calculation of periods — Periods and deadlines stated in this Law are calculated according to the Hijri calendar.
