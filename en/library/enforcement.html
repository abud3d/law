Article 1

Definitions — The Law defines terms including the Law, its implementing regulation, the Supreme Judicial Council, the Ministry of Justice, the Minister and the Enforcement Court.

Arabic title: التنفيذ

Article 65

Commencement and replacement — This Law replaces the previous Enforcement Law issued by Royal Decree No. (M/53) dated 13/8/1433H and repeals conflicting provisions. It comes into force 180 days after publication in the Official Gazette.
