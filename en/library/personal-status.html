Personal Status Law

Covers marriage, spousal rights, divorce, khul', custody, guardianship, wills and inheritance.

Publication date: 18 March 2022
Source status: In force according to the official portal
Official authority: Bureau of Experts at the Council of Ministers
Official text language: Arabic

Selected indexed provisions

Article 1

Engagement — Engagement is a request for marriage and a promise to marry.

Arabic title: الأحوال الشخصية

Article 42

Rights of spouses — Each spouse has rights toward the other, including kind treatment, mutual respect, avoiding material or moral harm, protecting the family's interests and caring for children.

Arabic title: الأحوال الشخصية
