Labor Law

Regulates employment relationships, contracts, and the rights and duties of workers and employers.

Publication date: 28 October 2005
Source status: In force according to the official portal
Official authority: Bureau of Experts at the Council of Ministers
Official text language: Arabic

Article 1

Name — This Law is called the Labor Law.

Article 9

Language of records — Arabic is the required language for data, records, files and employment contracts covered by this Law. Where another language is used alongside Arabic, the Arabic text prevails.
